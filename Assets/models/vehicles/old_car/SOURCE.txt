This folder holds a derivative: the model was split into parts (segmented), then split further into loose pieces,
levelled, scaled to a 4.6 m car and exported to one OBJ/vmdl per part. Textures are the original atlas.

  parts/                  one model per part (naming and positions in parts_manifest.json)
  old_car_static.vmdl     everything that never moves, merged (what the game renders)
  old_car_assembled.vmdl  the whole car in one model, for reference
